# Dataset

Every event in `events.py` is a real, publicly reported fact — manually compiled from company newsrooms and tech press, not scraped and not synthetic. Nothing here is generated or guessed; the goal was to make sure Timeline IQ's memory reflects things that actually happened, since both companies are real and the project is intended to be shared publicly.

## Known caveats

A few entries carry more uncertainty than the rest, worth checking before citing them publicly:

- **Galaxy S26 pricing** was deliberately left out of the headline — sources disagreed on whether Samsung held or slightly raised prices for this launch.
- **Galaxy Book6 pricing** reflects UK listings; some Korean listings reported prices roughly 20% higher.
- **The foldable iPhone's name and price** ("iPhone Duo," $1,999) come from post-event recap coverage rather than Apple's own materials, and the naming varied by outlet.
