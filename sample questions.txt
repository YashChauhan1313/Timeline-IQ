SAMPLE QUESTIONS — Timeline IQ
(Tailored to events.py: Samsung Galaxy S/Z/Book/Tab lines and Apple iPhone/MacBook/iPad
lines, 2020-2026)

General / cross-category
- How has Samsung evolved across phones, laptops, and tablets?
- How has Apple's product lineup changed over the past two years?
- Compare Samsung's tablet strategy to Apple's.

Pricing-focused
- What's the starting price for Samsung's latest phone lineup?
- How does Apple's laptop pricing compare to its tablet pricing?
- Has Samsung's flagship phone gotten more expensive over time?

Category-specific
- What's new in Samsung's laptop lineup?
- What's new in Apple's tablet lineup?
- Summarize Apple's phone launches over the last two years.
- How has Samsung's tablet strategy evolved?

Strategy / narrative (best for showing off reflect())
- What does Samsung's recent lineup suggest about their AI strategy?
- What's the biggest theme across Samsung's 2025-2026 launches?
- If you had to brief an executive on Apple in 60 seconds, what would you say?

Compare mode (LLM analyst, both companies — needs "compare" mode selected)
- Which company launched more devices in 2025?
- Compare Samsung's and Apple's pricing across categories.
- Who has moved faster on AI features, Samsung or Apple?
- Compare how often each company refreshes its laptop lineup.

Demo tip: ask a broad one first ("How has Samsung evolved...") to show the full
synthesized narrative, then a narrower follow-up ("What about just laptops?") to
show it can scope down. Then switch to compare mode for a head-to-head question —
that three-step arc (broad -> narrow -> compare) is what sells the memory story
to judges.
